Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

At SnakeZilla, we care deeply about the privacy and security of our visitors. This privacy policy explains what data we do not collect or collect while using our website (yilan-oyunlari.github.io).

1. Personal Data Collection

You do not need to create an account, register, or share any personal information (name, email address, phone number, etc.) to play our games. Our website does not request or collect any personal data directly from its visitors.

2. Local Storage

High score records and game preferences on our platform are not stored on remote servers; they are stored entirely within your browser's local storage area. No one from outside can access this data. When you clear your browser history or site data, your scores are permanently deleted from your device.

3. Cookies and Analytics

To measure the performance of our site, detect errors, and provide a better gaming experience to our visitors, we may use standard analytical cookies (e.g., Google Analytics). These cookies collect anonymous site traffic data and do not reveal your personal identity. By using our website, you are considered to have accepted this basic cookie usage.

4. Third-Party Links

Our website may occasionally contain links to other websites. These sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for the content or security policies of these sites.

5. Contact

If you have any questions, suggestions, or concerns regarding our privacy policy, you can reach out to us via the channels provided on our Contact page.
