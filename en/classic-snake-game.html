How to Play Classic Snake Game?

The Classic Snake Game is a timeless arcade legend familiar from arcades and first-generation computers. The rules are simple: grow your snake as much as possible by eating the glowing food that appears on the screen and break your own high score record.

Game Tactics

  • Watch the Walls: If the snake's head hits the screen boundaries or its own tail, the game is over.
  • Move Strategically: As your snake gets longer, it will take up more space on the screen. Draw zigzags using the corners.
  • Speed Control: You constantly move forward, so always plan your next move in advance.
