How to Succeed in Snake Maze Mode?

Instead of wide and open spaces, the Snake Maze mode, set in narrow corridors divided by cyber architectural walls, is a true marvel of geometry and timing. You must adjust your turning times with millimeter precision.

Architecture Changing by Level

While Level 1 has a basic wall structure, the maze becomes more complex and the corridors narrow every 5 points (Level 2 and beyond). Collecting food without hitting the walls requires true professionalism and superior reflexes.
