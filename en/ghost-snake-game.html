Ghost Snake Game Challenge

A hardcore mode designed only for the most experienced and professional players: Ghost Snake Game. In this mode, as your snake grows, the trailing parts of its body and tail become completely invisible (transparent).

Track Your Invisible Body

Even though your tail is not visible on the screen, it is physically still there and open to collisions. Therefore, to avoid hitting your own body, you must keep the route you took in the last few seconds alive in your mind and move by memorizing your turns. This mode tests not only your reflexes but also your short-term memory!
